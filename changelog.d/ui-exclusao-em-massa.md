### Added

- You can delete several agents and channels at once. Choose Select in the menu of a chat in the
  sidebar, or Ctrl-click (Cmd-click on macOS) a chat. Shift-click picks every chat between two.
  Then choose Delete in the bar under the list. The agents leave with one Undo for all of them, and
  the channels move to Deleted channels, where you can restore them. Escape or Cancel drops the
  selection.
