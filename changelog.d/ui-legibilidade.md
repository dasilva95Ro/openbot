### Added

- You can read the full thinking of an agent's turn in a dialog. Click the expand button on the
  activity line while the agent works, or choose Show full thinking in the menu of a finished
  answer, also in a thread. The dialog
  shows each thought in full, with its paragraphs, and the progress lines between them, in time
  order. While the turn runs, it updates live and follows the newest text until you scroll up.
- You can choose the color of the small icons in Settings › Appearance › Icon color: the sidebar,
  the actions beside each message and the menus. Choose a preset or any color. Default keeps the
  theme's colors. The choice applies on this device.

### Changed

- The steps list under the activity line is taller and scrolls while the agent works. Before, it
  showed only the newest steps, and older thoughts went out of view.
- Thoughts in the steps list are easier to read: they are no longer italic, and they have more
  contrast.
